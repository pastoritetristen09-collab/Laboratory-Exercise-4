How does insertion work?

A node only knows about the next node. To put a new node in the middle, we just re-connect two arrows. Example: insert Cider at position 2 into Pumpkin ➜ Acorn.

  1. Walk from the head to the node just before the spot (position 1, Pumpkin).
  2. Hook the new node forward: Cider's next = Pumpkin's old next (Acorn).
  3. Hook the old node to the new one: Pumpkin's next = Cider.

Pumpkin ➜ Cider ➜ Acorn
