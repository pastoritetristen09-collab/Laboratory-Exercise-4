Push, pop, and peek

This stack is built from linked nodes. Push adds on top, pop removes the top, peek just looks at the top.

push: the new node's next points to the old top, then top moves to the new node.

pop: save the top node, move top down to top.next, then return the saved data.

The rules (shunting yard)
  1. Read the expression left to right.
  2. Operand (letter or number): add it to the output.
  3. (: push it on the ops stack.
  4. ): pop operators to the output until you reach (, then throw the ( away.
  5. Operator: while the top of the ops stack has higher precedence (or equal, for left-associative operators), pop it to the output. Then push the new operator.
  6. At the end, pop everything left on the ops stack to the output.

Precedence: ^ is 3 (right associative), * / are 2, + - are 1 (left associative).
